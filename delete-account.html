ThreatQuest allows users to permanently delete their account and associated cloud data. To delete your account: 1. Open ThreatQuest. 2. Go to Account → Settings. 3. Scroll to Danger Zone. 4. Tap Delete Account. 5. Confirm the deletion. The following data will be permanently removed: • Authentication account • Email address (if applicable) • Training history • XP • Achievements • Leaderboard profile • Cloud-saved statistics Deletion is permanent and cannot be undone.
