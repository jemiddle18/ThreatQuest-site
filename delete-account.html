Delete Your ThreatQuest Account

ThreatQuest allows users with an account to permanently delete their ThreatQuest account and associated cloud data directly from the app.

How to Delete Your Account

  1. Open the ThreatQuest app.
  2. Navigate to the Account screen.
  3. Scroll down to the Danger Zone.
  4. Tap Delete Account.
  5. Review the warning and confirm deletion.

Data Deleted With Your Account

Deleting your ThreatQuest account removes associated account data maintained by ThreatQuest, including:

  • Your ThreatQuest authentication account
  • Email address associated with the account, if applicable
  • Cloud-saved training progress and history
  • XP and rank progress
  • Achievements
  • Streak and training statistics
  • Public leaderboard profile and leaderboard participation
  • Other ThreatQuest cloud data associated with your account

Important Information

Account deletion is permanent. Once your account has been deleted, ThreatQuest cannot restore the deleted account or its associated cloud progress.

If you only want to clear your training progress without deleting your account, use the Reset Progress option in ThreatQuest instead.

Certain information may be retained where necessary for legal compliance, security, fraud prevention, or technical reasons, as described in our Privacy Policy.

ThreatQuest Pro Subscriptions

Deleting your ThreatQuest account does not automatically cancel an active subscription.

If you subscribe to ThreatQuest Pro through Apple, you must separately cancel your subscription through your Apple Account to prevent future renewals.

You can manage your subscription on your device by opening Settings, selecting your Apple Account, and choosing Subscriptions.

For additional instructions, visit Apple Subscription Support .

Need Help?

If you have questions about account deletion, contact ThreatQuest through the in-app feedback or support options.

For more information about how ThreatQuest handles your information, review our Privacy Policy.
