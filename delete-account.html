Delete Your ThreatQuest Account

ThreatQuest allows users with an account to permanently delete their ThreatQuest account and associated cloud data directly from the app.

How to delete your account

  1. Open ThreatQuest.
  2. Open the Account screen.
  3. Scroll to the Danger Zone.
  4. Tap Delete Account.
  5. Review the warning and confirm the deletion.

Data deleted with your account

Deleting your ThreatQuest account removes associated account data, including:

  • Your ThreatQuest authentication account
  • Email address associated with the account, if applicable
  • Cloud-saved training progress and history
  • XP and rank progress
  • Achievements
  • Streak and training statistics
  • Public leaderboard profile and leaderboard participation
  • Other ThreatQuest cloud data associated with your account

Important

Account deletion is permanent and cannot be undone. Once your account has been deleted, ThreatQuest cannot restore the deleted account or its associated cloud progress.

If you only want to clear your training progress without deleting your account, use the Reset Progress option in ThreatQuest instead.

Need help?

If you have questions about account deletion, contact ThreatQuest through the in-app feedback or support options.
