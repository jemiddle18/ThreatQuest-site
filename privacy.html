Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 2026

ThreatQuest respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information may be collected when you use ThreatQuest, how that information is used, and the choices available to you.

Information We Collect

The information ThreatQuest processes depends on how you use the app. If you create or sign in to an account, account information may include your email address, authentication provider, user ID, display information, leaderboard name, and account preferences.

ThreatQuest also stores training-related information such as XP, ranks, achievements, streaks, quiz and training history, performance statistics, learning recommendations, and other progress information needed to provide app features.

Local Training Data

ThreatQuest may be used without signing in. When you use the app without an account, training progress and related preferences may be stored locally on your device.

Local-only progress may not be recoverable if the app is deleted, app data is cleared, or the device is lost or replaced unless that progress has been associated with a supported account and synchronized.

Accounts and Cloud Sync

If you sign in using a supported account provider, such as Apple or Google, certain account and training information may be stored using cloud services to support features such as progress synchronization, account recovery, and use across supported devices.

Public Leaderboards

Participation in the public ThreatQuest leaderboard is optional. If you choose to join, information associated with your public leaderboard profile may be visible to other users. This may include your chosen leaderboard name, XP, rank, streak, and selected training statistics.

Your email address and other private account information are not intended to be included in your public leaderboard profile.

How We Use Information

ThreatQuest uses information to provide and maintain training features, track progress, calculate XP and ranks, award achievements, maintain streaks, personalize learning recommendations, synchronize eligible account data, operate optional leaderboard features, troubleshoot issues, and improve the learning experience.

Analytics and App Usage

ThreatQuest uses Firebase Analytics, a Google service, to help understand how the app is used and improve the training experience.

Analytics information may include app interactions, training session activity, selected training modes or categories, achievement activity, account creation events, leaderboard participation events, device and app information, and similar usage data.

ThreatQuest does not intentionally send email addresses, leaderboard names, quiz question text, quiz answers, or other user-entered content to Firebase Analytics as custom analytics event data.

Analytics information is used for purposes such as measuring feature usage, evaluating app performance, identifying areas for improvement, and understanding how users interact with ThreatQuest.

Service Providers

ThreatQuest does not sell your personal information. We use third-party service providers that may process information as necessary to operate and improve the app.

These providers may include Google Firebase services for authentication, cloud data storage, synchronization, analytics, and related application functionality; supported sign-in providers such as Apple and Google; and RevenueCat for subscription management, purchase status, and access to paid features.

Data Retention and Deletion

Account and training information may be retained while needed to provide ThreatQuest features and maintain your account.

ThreatQuest provides options within the app to reset training progress and, where applicable, delete your account.

Deleting an account is intended to remove the associated ThreatQuest account data and public leaderboard profile maintained by ThreatQuest, subject to information that may need to be retained for legal, security, fraud-prevention, or technical purposes.

For information about account deletion, including available deletion options, please visit the ThreatQuest Account Deletion page .

Data Security

ThreatQuest uses reasonable technical and organizational measures designed to protect information processed through the app.

However, no method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Your Choices

You may use supported portions of ThreatQuest without signing in, choose whether to participate in the public leaderboard, manage supported app preferences, reset training progress, and delete your account where available.

Certain data processing may also be controlled through device, operating system, or service-provider privacy settings where applicable.

Feedback and Support

Information you voluntarily provide through feedback or support features may be used to respond to your request, investigate reported issues, and improve ThreatQuest.

Children's Privacy

ThreatQuest is not directed to children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

If we learn that such information has been collected, we will take appropriate steps to delete it.

Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated as ThreatQuest evolves. When material changes are made, the date at the top of this page will be updated.

Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or your information, you may contact us through the in-app feedback or support options.
