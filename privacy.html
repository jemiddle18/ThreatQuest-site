PhishSmart Privacy Policy

Last Updated: July 2026

Information We Collect

PhishSmart may collect your email address, authentication provider, user ID, leaderboard name, and training progress when you choose to create an account.

Training information such as XP, achievements, streaks, quiz history, leaderboard statistics, and personalized learning recommendations may be stored to provide app functionality and synchronize progress across devices.

How We Use Information

Information is used solely to provide cybersecurity awareness training, synchronize progress, personalize learning, improve the app, and operate leaderboards.

Data Sharing

PhishSmart does not sell or rent your personal information. Information is only shared with trusted service providers necessary to operate the application, such as Firebase authentication and cloud storage services.

Security

Information transmitted between your device and our cloud services is encrypted using industry-standard security protocols.

Your Choices

You may use Guest mode, sign in using Apple or Google, or request that your cloud account be removed by contacting us.

Contact

Questions regarding this Privacy Policy may be submitted using the feedback option within the app.
